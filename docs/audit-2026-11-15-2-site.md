# Serve Funding website: content audit

Scope: `/tmp/audit/website` (branch dev, last commit 2026-09-29). Every route under `src/app`, every file in `src/data`, all 32 posts. Read only, nothing edited. Word counts are estimates (code and markup stripped by eye; data-driven pages are the data file divided by the number of pages). "Last updated" comes from `src/data/last-updated.generated.ts` (git dates) or post frontmatter.

Privacy: no client names. Case studies are described by pattern only. Dollar amounts below are company-level facts or CRM aggregates, not tied to a client.

## 0. The short version

1. The site is thorough but the home page does not say plainly what Serve does until you scroll. The first screen is three rotating slogans.
2. The site's own proof claims are the weakest part. "100+ clients", "65% repeat rate" and "3-10 days" are all in the code marked unverified, and the CRM contradicts or cannot support each one.
3. The most useful proof (candour, "we will tell you when it is the wrong answer", one point of contact) is on deep pages, not on the home page.
4. There are no client testimonials anywhere. The only quotes are three anonymous banker/advisor quotes on /partners.
5. Many numbers disagree between pages (speed, size floors, payroll example, case totals). Section 2 lists them.

### 2.2 Items still marked `[VERIFY]` or unverified in `company-info.ts`

The whole file is headed "PENDING FOUNDER VERIFICATION" with `Last Updated: [DATE]` unfilled. Specific flags: founding date and company age; total employees ("5"); clients served and repeat rate ("NOT yet founder-verified"); every section header says "VERIFY WITH FOUNDER"; qualification criteria say "VERIFY WITH LENDER PARTNERS" and several fields are empty strings (`minimumAssets`, `minimumRevenue`, `minimumTimeInBusiness`, `education`). The `verificationChecklist` asks for testimonials, NPS and "success metrics documented", and none exist.

These values feed the chatbot prompt, JSON-LD and both llms files, so unverified numbers reach AI search engines even where the visible page avoids them. `llms-full.txt:136-144` prints the 100+, 65% and 3-10 day claims as "Key Metrics".

### 2.3 What the CRM supports, contradicts, or cannot speak to

- **Supports:** $50MM+ capital, founded 2021 (closings begin 2022), Southeast focus, banker referrals as top source, construction/healthcare/manufacturing as core industries, a large lender network.

- **Cannot speak to:** 15+ years, team size, success-fee terms, refinance savings percentages, cost-of-capital reduction, TMA membership, charity giving, per-product timing, whether several lenders really bid.

## 3. Story and structure

### 3.1 Does the home page say what Serve does, for whom, and why trust it?

**What it does.** Not plainly, not above the fold. The first thing a visitor sees is a rotating hero (`page.tsx:67-87`): "Funding Solutions to Serve Healthy Business Growth", "Creative Capital Empowering Entrepreneurs", "You Value Relationships Over Quick Fixes". None says "we are an advisor, not a lender; we find you a lender when your bank says no". The clear sentence ("not a lender", "when banks decline") lives in the FAQ at the bottom (`faq-data.ts:52`) and in metadata the visitor never reads. "Creative working capital" appears three times and is never defined.

**For whom.** Partly. "The Bank Says No" is in a bullet list halfway down (`page.tsx:~185`). The 10-bullet list mixes situations (bank says no) with products (bridge) with events (M&A). No size or stage statement ("businesses with $X revenue, 2+ years") appears on the home page, though `/bankers` has a clean table (`bankers/page.tsx:297`). The tone is aimed at "entrepreneurs"; the CRM says most work arrives by referral from bankers and repeat clients.

**Why trust.** Weak. The "Transparent, Timely, Trusted" trio is a slogan, not evidence. The home page has no number, no client voice, no named lender count, no "not a lender" line, no fee line, and no photo of a person. The trust proof sits on the funded-case grid (6 of 19 cases, `fundingCases.slice(0,6)`), which is good, but each card is cropped at 6em of text so the specifics are hidden. "Meet the Team" has no names. There are no testimonials on the site except three anonymous partner quotes on `/partners`.

**Real proof that exists but is buried:**
- 19 case studies with real structure and honest timelines (`/fundings`).
- "When this is the wrong answer" and candour passages on product pages and posts (Michael's voice, and the exact thing owners thank him for in `quotes-findings.md`).
- Success-fee-only terms (`faq-data.ts:72`) which answer the "will you charge me before I see anything" fear. It is not on the home page.
- Memberships (ACG, SFNet, IFA) appear only as footer logos.

**Home page order today:** hero carousel, 3 slogans, CTA band, 10 bullets, 12 product cards, 15 industry tiles, 3-step process, 6 cases, 8 FAQs, team link, CTA. Twelve sections, two identical CTA bands ("Capital That Serves You" and "Ready to Get Started?") with near-identical copy.

### 3.3 Structure problems

- Three audience pages (`/partners`, `/bankers`, `/advisors`) overlap. `/partners` is a general pitch with three anonymous quotes; `/bankers` is a deep page; `/advisors` is "Business Advisors: Resource & Referral Hub". They share the same promise ("introduction, 20-minute call, options") and numbers. The footer lists all three beside "Fundings" and "Discover" with no explanation.
- `/solutions/compare` (12 products) and `/compare` (5 head-to-heads) are two comparison systems.
- 12 products x 6 industries x 5 comparisons x 33 glossary x 168 FAQs x 32 posts creates a lot of surface for a team of five, and each repeats numbers.
