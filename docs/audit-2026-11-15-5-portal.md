# Portal copy audit: client and lender facing text

Scope: default templates (`src/lib/settings/defaults.ts`), live overrides (`/tmp/audit/org_email_templates.json`, these win over defaults), client application pages, login and signup, lender portal, reminders. Portal root: `/Users/timburnham/conductor/workspaces/portal/brazzaville`. Nothing was edited.

Voice yardstick: Mike's profile says every email ends "Here to serve," and he opens external mail with "Good morning/afternoon [Name]," or "Hi [Name],". In his 400 recent sent emails, "Here to serve" is in 362 (90%). "Thank you" is in 254 (64%). "Friendly nudge" or "friendly reminder" appears once. "Touch base" appears twice. The brand guide bans "bank-speak" and says "Relationships over bots" (the site description says the same).

Not verified: I could not tell which tenant settings are live beyond the one overrides file. Where I say "live", I mean the text in `org_email_templates.json`. Only 7 of the 16 template keys are overridden there (invite, approval, reminder, offer_to_client, lender_criteria_nudge, client_application_invite, client_application_continue). The other 9 keys run on code defaults.

## Pattern across templates

1. No default template ends "Here to serve," (it appears only in the live `offer_to_client` override). Sign-offs in use are "Thanks,", "Best,", "Best regards,", "Appreciate it,", "{{BRAND_NAME}} Team", or bare "{{BRAND_NAME}}". Mike's profile calls the tagline non-optional, and he used it in 90% of recent mail.
2. Several client emails are signed by the brand rather than a person, though the app already knows `{{FROM_NAME}}`. Mike's emails are personal.
3. Stock phrases ("friendly nudge", "Quick favor", "outstanding", "Appreciate it") that Mike's profile lists as filler or does not use.
4. Greeting is always "Hi {{FIRST_NAME}}," which fits his neutral opener; leave it.

## Top 10 fixes

1. Remove the engagement agreement from the client invite (live override and default). It promises a step the client no longer sees (#1).
2. Fix the founder letter sentence on signing so it matches the flow (#4).
3. Drop or confirm "within one business day" (#5).
4. Add "Here to serve," plus `{{FROM_NAME}}` to every client and lender template, defaults and overrides (pattern 1, #3, #6, #8, #11, #16-19, #28).
5. Take "AI-native" out of the lender invite and reminder, and confirm or remove "Listings for lenders are free" (#9, #10).
6. Rewrite the lender reminder so it asks once and restores `{{MISSING_FIELDS}}` (#11).
7. Correct the acceptance email: subject, body and the "sent automatically" description (#7, #8).
8. Fix the invite wording "your {{REQUESTED_AMOUNT}} Serve application" and the hardcoded "great to meet you" (#2).
9. Fix the lender approval subject: grammar, and "accepted" versus "reviewed" (#12).
10. Fix the login footer range, font and brand colour, and settle the "$250" versus "$250K" typo with the site (#24). Also move the hardcoded Calendly link out of the nudge template (#13).
