# Advance rates to verify before /funding goes live

For Michael. Fifteen minutes. Last updated 2026-08-28.

These numbers came from general market knowledge, **not** from Serve's lender matrix. They
are the most quotable figures on the eight new pages — an AI assistant answering "what
advance rate can a manufacturer get on finished goods" will repeat them to a prospect
verbatim. A wrong one is worse than not publishing the page.

For each: **confirm**, **correct it**, or say **"varies too much to publish"** and it gets
widened or cut. Anything you correct, I change in one file.

## The six that matter most

Check these even if you skip everything below.

| # | Claim | Page | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finished goods advance at **50%–65% of net orderly liquidation value** | ABL manufacturing | Terms table + direct answer |
| 2 | Raw materials advance at **25%–50%** | ABL manufacturing | Terms table + direct answer |
| 3 | Work in process is **ineligible, zero borrowing base** | ABL manufacturing | Direct answer, terms table, FAQ, worked example |
| 4 | Healthcare advances at **65%–75% of net collectible value**, not of billed charges | ABL healthcare | Direct answer, terms table, FAQ |
| 5 | Construction retainage **excluded, or advanced at 0%–25%** | ABL construction | Terms table, deflection, FAQ |
| 6 | Staffing AR advances at **85%–90%** | ABL staffing | Direct answer, terms table, FAQ |

Number 3 is the one I would most want a second opinion on. It appears four times on the
manufacturing page and the whole worked example is built on it.

a glance for anything obviously wrong.
