# 1. GEO (AI answer engines): what is rewarded, and how servefunding.com measures up

Written 2026-11-15. Research was done with web search and a few page fetches. Where I only saw a vendor summary, I say so. Where I am unsure, it is marked **[unsure]**. No repo was edited.

Short version: the site's technical base is good and ahead of most small advisors. The biggest gaps are not technical. They are (1) our own numbers disagree with each other and with the CRM, (2) almost nothing about Serve exists outside servefunding.com, and (3) Mike's real first-hand material (video, data, deal experience) is not yet turned into things an engine can cite.

---

## (a) What current GEO practice says (dated, sourced)

**What the platforms themselves say**

- Google, optimisation guide for AI Overviews / AI Mode (May 2026 per press coverage; the page itself shows no date). It says this is "still SEO". A page only needs to be indexed and snippet-eligible. Do: non-commodity content with a "unique point of view" (first-hand experience, expert insight), clear headings, crawlable pages, good page experience. Ignore: llms.txt ("Google Search itself doesn't use them"), chunking content, "AEO/GEO hacks", rewriting for AI, and chasing inauthentic off-site mentions. Structured data is not required and no special schema exists for AI. Mass pages for query variations can fall under the scaled-content spam policy. https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide
- Google, "AI features and your website". Same eligibility rule, no extra technical requirement. AI features use "query fan-out" (many related searches per question). https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
- Google, 3 Jun 2026: Search Console is getting a control for whether a site is used in generative AI features, and a generative AI performance report. Rolling out to a subset (UK first) per coverage. I saw this only via search summaries, so check it. https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/new-opportunities-control-and-insights-for-website-owners/
- OpenAI, crawler docs (undated page). Three agents with independent robots.txt settings. OAI-SearchBot decides whether you appear in ChatGPT search, and OpenAI says to allow it and its published IPs. GPTBot is training only. ChatGPT-User is user-triggered, "robots.txt rules may not apply". Changes take about 24 hours. https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots
- Perplexity, crawler docs. PerplexityBot indexes for search and is not used for training. Perplexity-User fetches on a person's request and generally ignores robots.txt. I did not read the full official page, only summaries. https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/perplexitybot
- Anthropic, help article on its crawlers. Three bots with separate tokens: ClaudeBot (training), Claude-User (user fetch), Claude-SearchBot (search index). Older `Claude-Web` and `anthropic-ai` are deprecated. Blocking SearchBot can reduce visibility in Claude search. Seen via snippets and secondary write-ups. https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518
- Bing Webmaster Guidelines, updated 27 Feb 2026 (SEJ report). GEO is now named in the guidelines (making content eligible to be grounded and cited). New sections on "Artificially Engineered Language" and "Prompt Injection and AI Manipulation". Large-scale content without editorial oversight may be excluded from the index. https://www.searchenginejournal.com/bing-adds-geo-to-official-guidelines-expands-ai-abuse-definitions/
- Bing Webmaster Tools "AI Performance" report, public preview Feb 2026. Shows citations in Copilot and Bing AI summaries by page and by grounding query. No click data. Microsoft only. Secondary sources. https://searchengineland.com/bing-webmaster-tools-testing-new-ai-performance-report-468039
- Microsoft Advertising, "From Discovery to Influence: a guide to AEO and GEO", Jan 2026, written for retailers. Advice is largely classic SEO plus structured data and visible review volume. Commentators say little is new. https://www.seroundtable.com/microsoft-advertiser-guide-to-aeo-geo-40748.html

**What studies find (mostly vendor studies; treat as directional)**

- Ahrefs, 12 Dec 2025, 75,000 brands. Strongest correlation with being mentioned by ChatGPT, AI Mode and AI Overviews: YouTube mentions (about 0.74), then branded web mentions (0.66 to 0.71). Backlinks and page count were weak. Correlation, not cause. ChatGPT leaned least on classic authority, so it is a possible entry point for smaller brands. https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations/
- Princeton / KDD 2024 GEO paper (arXiv 2311.09735). Adding statistics, citing sources and adding quotations raised visibility in generative answers by up to about 40%. Keyword stuffing did worst. The abstract does not list the methods; the detail is from secondary write-ups. Results vary by topic. It was a 2023-24 test set, not today's engines. https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735
- llms.txt. SE Ranking, about 300,000 domains: only about 10% have one, and it had no correlation with citations (removing it made their model more accurate). Other vendor studies find AI search crawlers rarely request it. Google says it does not use it. No independent evidence it helps. https://seranking.com/blog/llms-txt/
- FAQ markup. Google stopped showing FAQ rich results (7 May 2026 per secondary sources). The type is still valid. Vendor claims that it boosts AI citations are unproven; one cited study found no effect. Use it only on real Q&A content. https://www.quattr.com/blog/faq-schema-in-2026 (vendor summary)
- Freshness. Seer Interactive (5,000+ cited URLs) found AI engines favour recent content. A small On-Page.ai test found date-only bumps were cited as often as real updates but had less new information. Real updates matter; date games do not clearly pay. https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/study-ai-brand-visibility-and-content-recency
- Self-promotional "best X" lists. Peec, 232,000 citations (Dec 2025 to Feb 2026): about 1 in 10 citations on AI Mode and Perplexity, about 4% on ChatGPT. Lily Ray's test found AI Overviews often cite the list and then recommend a competitor. https://peec.ai/blog/self-promotional-listicles-analysis-from-232k-citations
- Finance sources. No clean study. Vendor audits show NerdWallet, Forbes, Investopedia, Wikipedia, YouTube and Reddit all appearing; Yext found most financial-services citations come from brand-controlled pages (88%, vendor claim). Directional only. https://www.yext.com/blog/2025/11/ai-relies-on-brand-managed-sources-for-88-percent-of-financial-services-citations
- Reviews. Google's rule since 2019: a business's own reviews of itself on its own site do not get review stars under LocalBusiness/Organization markup. https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2019/09/making-review-rich-results-more-helpful

**What is now spam or wasted effort**

- Mass pages for every question variant; thin AI-written volume (Google scaled-content policy; Bing "no editorial oversight").
- Keyword stuffing and engineered phrasing built to trigger citations; hidden text or instructions aimed at models (Bing prompt-injection rule).
- Buying or faking mentions and reviews; self-review markup.
- Date bumping with no real change.
- Chunk-size rules ("300 to 600 tokens") and special AI files as a ranking lever. Google says no on both; llms.txt has no evidence.
- Treating FAQ schema as a growth lever.

## (b) What the site already does well

1. **Crawl access is right.** `public/robots.txt` allows OAI-SearchBot (with a correct comment that GPTBot is not a substitute), GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Bingbot and others. Nothing is blocked.
2. **Bing and ChatGPT are fed on purpose.** `.github/workflows/indexnow.yml` pushes changed URLs to IndexNow on each main push. Sitemap `lastmod` comes from git, not the build date (`scripts/generate-last-updated.ts`, `src/app/sitemap.ts`). This follows the evidence.
3. **Answer-first, structured pages.** Comparison pages (`src/data/comparisons.ts`) are built answer-first with a 6 to 10 row table, "when to pick A / B", a worked example and Mike's own words. Solutions carry terms, worked examples and a "when this is the wrong answer" list, rendered into `src/app/llms-full.txt/route.ts`. The "wrong answer" part is honest and not self-promotional, which is the safe side of the listicle finding.
4. **First-hand voice.** Mike's verbatim explanations are used across comparisons, industries and the glossary. That matches Google's "unique point of view" advice.
5. **Honest schema in the main.** `src/lib/schema-generators.ts` has Organization/FinancialService with `memberOf`, `sameAs`, founder. `getReviewSchema` and `getAggregateRatingSchema` exist but are not used on any page (checked by grep), so no self-review markup is shipping. Glossary has DefinedTermSet (`src/app/glossary/page.tsx:41-70`). Blog posts have BlogPosting with real `datePublished` / `dateModified`.
6. **Blog dates are honest.** `lastUpdated` in frontmatter is required for a real edit (CLAUDE.md). Good against the date-bump trap.
7. **Fee figures are not published** (CLAUDE.md decision). Fine as a business call. See proposal 10 for the trade-off.
8. **Some outside sources are cited** in posts (Federal Reserve Small Business Credit Survey, `posts/working-capital-seasonal-businesses.mdoc:29`, `posts/how-to-choose-working-capital-lender.mdoc:33`). Good start; see proposal 5.

## (c) Gaps and proposals, ranked by expected impact

### 1. Make our own numbers agree with each other and with the CRM (highest impact)
Engines compare sources. When two pages on one site, or the site and a third party, give different numbers, the entity looks unreliable and the claim is dropped or hedged. Found:
- Speed. Site says "3 to 10 business days" (`public/llms.txt`, `src/app/llms-full.txt/route.ts:~150`). The CRM shows a median of about 54 days from deal created to funded (`/tmp/audit/portal_aggregates.json`). **[unsure]** These may measure different things (first term sheet vs funded; created date vs first call). Define each one on the page.
- ABL speed. `public/llms.txt` says 10 to 20 days; `src/data/faq-data.ts` (`top-how-fast-funding`, line ~61) says 4 to 8 weeks.
- Repeat clients. Site says 65% (`src/data/company-info.ts:50`). CRM: 6 of 57 funded clients have two or more funded deals; 21 of 66 funded deals have "Repeat Client" as lead source. These do not obviously support 65%. **[unsure]** on definition; the file itself is marked "PENDING FOUNDER VERIFICATION" (line 8).
- Clients served. "100+" vs 57 distinct funded clients in the CRM. May count unfunded advisory work. **[unsure]**.
- Lender network. FAQ says "dozens" (`faq-data.ts` ~109) while the CRM has 179 active lenders and 315 lender products. Other copy says "extensive". Pick one true figure.
- Seven open conflicts already listed in `docs/numeric-conflicts-for-michael.md` (factoring ceiling $100MM vs $25MM is the worst).
Action: Mike confirms each number once; set it only in `src/data/company-info.ts` and render it into FAQs, solutions, llms files. Add a `verify-seo`-style check that fails the build if a hardcoded copy of a registered number differs. Files: `src/data/company-info.ts`, `src/data/faq-data.ts`, `src/data/solutions.tsx`, `public/llms.txt`.

### 2. Publish first-party data that nobody else has
Google asks for non-commodity content; the Princeton work favours statistics; Ahrefs says to earn mentions. We hold real deal data. Publish one "What we see in funded deals" page or post with methodology and a date: funded mix by industry (construction and healthcare lead), request-size buckets, source of deals (bankers and repeat clients dominate), recorded closings, real time-to-fund with both definitions. Counts only, no client detail, nothing identifying. Refresh yearly with a true `lastUpdated`. This is also the page other sites (bankers' associations, CPAs) can cite. Files: new page or `posts/*.mdoc`; add to `src/app/llms-full.txt/route.ts` and `src/app/sitemap.ts`. Needs Mike's sign-off on what to disclose. Small sample (66 funded deals), so state it plainly.

### 3. Put Mike's video on YouTube, with transcripts, and link it to the site
Ahrefs: YouTube mentions were the strongest single correlate of AI visibility (correlation only). `HIGH-INTENT-QUESTIONS-STRATEGY.md` says Mike records 2 to 3 videos a week, but only 2 FAQ entries carry a `videoId` (`src/data/faq-data.ts`; embed in `src/components/FAQSection.tsx`), no `VideoObject` schema exists, and `sameAs` has no YouTube channel. Do: one channel under the company name, titles that are the buyer's question, full transcript on the matching page, `VideoObject` markup, channel in `sameAs` (`src/lib/schema-generators.ts:~58`). Use his own words only, per site rules.

### 4. Build the off-site footprint (the part the site cannot do alone)
`sameAs` lists only a LinkedIn company page and Facebook (`schema-generators.ts:58-61`). Branded mentions on other sites correlate most strongly with AI mentions, and Google says fake ones do not help. Real ones, in order of ease: member directory pages at SFNet, IFA and ACG (the site already claims membership); a verified Google Business Profile (Atlanta address is in schema); Crunchbase and similar profiles; guest pieces and quotes in banker, CPA and trade publications; genuine Google reviews from funded clients, asked for with permission. Keep name, address, phone and the founder bio identical everywhere. Do not add `Review` or `AggregateRating` markup from our own site for these. Files: `src/lib/schema-generators.ts` (`sameAs`), `src/data/company-info.ts`.

### 5. Cite primary sources with links, and add attributed quotes
Many pages state figures with no source (rates, advance percentages, "typical" ranges in `src/data/solutions.tsx`, `comparisons.ts`, `glossary.ts`). The Fed survey is named in two posts but not linked. Add a short "Sources" line with links (Federal Reserve Small Business Credit Survey, SBA, Secured Finance Network, IFA) and mark which numbers are Serve's own experience vs market data. Keep Mike's quotes attributed by name and role. Backed by the Princeton paper (statistics, citations, quotations), with the caveat above on its age. Files: `posts/*.mdoc`, `src/data/solutions.tsx`, `src/data/comparisons.ts`.

### 6. Stop hand-maintaining `public/llms.txt`; do not spend more on it
It is a static 124-line file that has drifted: it links to `/funding` (now a permanent redirect to `/solutions/compare`, `next.config.ts:127`), describes "Funding by Problem" pages that were retired, and carries a different ABL speed than the FAQ. The evidence says it does not affect citations and Google ignores it. Cheapest fix: generate it from the same data as `src/app/llms-full.txt/route.ts` (or delete it). Also drop the `Last generated: <today>` stamp at `llms-full.txt/route.ts:122`; it is the "stamp today" pattern the repo's own freshness rules warn against. Low effort, low impact; do it only to remove inconsistency.

### 7. Make freshness real and visible per page
`dateModified` and `lastmod` for all solution pages come from one shared file (`src/data/solutions.tsx` via `scripts/generate-last-updated.ts:74`; `src/app/sitemap.ts:62-65`). Editing one solution moves the date of every solution page, which over-reports changes. Same for comparisons, industries and glossary. Give each entry its own `lastReviewed` date set by a human when numbers are rechecked, show "Reviewed by Michael Kodinsky, <date>" on the page, and derive schema and sitemap from that. This is the part of freshness evidence that survives scrutiny (real updates over date bumps). Files: `src/data/solutions.tsx`, `comparisons.ts`, `scripts/generate-last-updated.ts`, `src/app/sitemap.ts`.

### 8. Tie the schema together as one entity graph
Organization uses `@id: "https://servefunding.com"` (no fragment); the founder is an inline Person in the Organization and a separate Person on `/about-us`; blog authors are a new inline Person per post (`schema-generators.ts:221-265`, `about-us/page.tsx:69`). Give Organization and the founder stable `@id`s (`#org`, `#michael`) and reference them from BlogPosting `author`/`publisher`, the Person page, and every WebPage. Add `reviewedBy` / `author` on solution and comparison pages. Google says markup is not needed for AI features, so this is hygiene for entity clarity (Bing and others), not a lever. Keep FAQPage where there is real Q&A, but do not build more FAQ pages for the markup.

### 9. Measure it, so we stop guessing
Nothing in the repo measures AI visibility. Set up now, all free: Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance report (Copilot citations by page and query); Search Console generative AI report once available to us; server-log counts for OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot/Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot; a monthly manual check of 20 to 30 real buyer questions (use `CONTENT-QUESTIONS-MASTER.csv`) in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Mode, recording whether Serve is named, cited or absent and who is cited instead. Also record referral traffic from chat.openai.com, perplexity.ai and similar in analytics. Ahrefs Brand Radar is available in our tool list if a paid tracker is wanted.

### 10. Decide, deliberately, how to answer "what does it cost?"
Engines answer cost questions from somewhere. With no figure on our pages, they use third-party broker content. The site's choice to remove fee figures is a business decision (CLAUDE.md) and I am not recommending reversing it. A middle route that does not publish a number: say how the fee works in plain terms (paid by the lender or by the client, paid only at close), what it is compared against, and why a bad-fit advisor fee is a red flag. Current answer ("success fee upon closing...", `faq-data.ts` ~`top-how-much-cost`) is repeated near-verbatim in several places and says little. Mike decides.

### 11. Add a neutral "advisor vs going direct vs online marketplace" page
This is the question the call quotes show buyers arrive with (being chased by lenders after one form; see `/tmp/audit/quotes-findings.md`). A fair comparison that includes where Serve is not the right answer and discloses how Serve is paid is the safe form of a comparison page given the self-promo findings. Existing `/compare` pages are product vs product; this one is provider type vs provider type. Files: `src/data/comparisons.ts` (new entry), `src/app/sitemap.ts` auto-picks it up.

### 12. Retire or relabel the generic AIEO docs, and cap volume
`AIEO WAY.md` is a template written for a different product ("PulseAI") and teaches 300 to 600 token chunking, which Google says is unnecessary. `AIEO-CONTENT-UPDATE-STRATEGY.md` plans 15 to 20 FAQs and 2 to 4 comparison pages every week. At that rate the site drifts toward the scaled-content pattern Google and Bing both name, and it multiplies the number-consistency problem in proposal 1. Replace with a short rule: new pages only where Mike has a real answer from a real deal; one page per real question; every number from `company-info.ts`. Files: `AIEO WAY.md`, `AIEO-CONTENT-UPDATE-STRATEGY.md`.

### 13. Tidy `robots.txt` (low impact)
`public/robots.txt` lists the deprecated `anthropic-ai` and `Claude-Web` tokens and lacks `Claude-SearchBot` and `Perplexity-User`. Everything is allowed by default, so nothing is lost today; this only matters if a block is ever added. Also, a crawler with its own group (Googlebot, Bingbot, GPTBot...) does not inherit the `*` group, so the `Disallow: /admin/` and `/private/` lines apply to none of them. **[unsure]** whether those paths exist or are protected elsewhere (`middleware.ts`). Add the same Disallows to the named groups or drop the named groups.

## Not worth doing (so nobody spends time on it)
- Chasing llms.txt as a ranking lever; chunking pages; one page per query variant.
- More FAQ markup for its own sake; the `speakable` property on every page (`schema-generators.ts:70, 258`; I believe Google supports it only for news, **[unsure]**).
- Self-reviews or aggregate ratings in markup.
- Self-ranked "best funding company" lists.
- Buying mentions or links.

## Caveats on this research
Most numbers come from SEO vendors with a commercial interest, are correlations, and often come from secondary write-ups rather than the original study. Platform behaviour changes with model releases. The Google, OpenAI, Anthropic and Bing statements are the firmest items. Dates of some pages (Google guide, OpenAI and Perplexity docs) were not visible in my fetch.
